La Maison

Founded in the trenches.

WICK was founded in the trenches by traders who had seen enough red for one lifetime. We set up a small atelier with a single rule, and we have not broken it since: we only pour green.

Every candle cures for exactly one block. It is then inspected under natural light for any sign of red. Red ones are melted down and poured again. Nothing leaves the atelier looking like a loss.

We do not sell candles, and we never have. The candles are lore. The token is $WICK.
